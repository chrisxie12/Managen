Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is the free trial really free? ▾
Yes. The 7-day Free Trial gives you full access to the platform with no payment details required. You only provide billing information when you choose to upgrade to a paid plan.
Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime? ▾
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your school dashboard. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Your data is never deleted.
Does Managen work for the Ghanaian curriculum? ▾
Absolutely. Managen is built specifically for Ghanaian schools and fully supports the local curriculum including WAEC, BECE, and GES formats. Term reports are auto-generated in the approved GES format.
How does the WhatsApp integration work? ▾
Managen connects directly to the WhatsApp Business API. Once set up, attendance summaries, fee reminders, and exam results are sent automatically to parents — no manual effort from your staff.
Is my school data secure? ▾
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each school operates in its own isolated environment with regular backups. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.
Can I export my data if I leave? ▾
Yes. You can export all your data — student records, fees, grades, attendance — in standard formats (CSV, Excel, PDF) at any time. No lock-in. No hidden fees. Your data is yours.
What if I have multiple campuses? ▾
The Enterprise plan includes multi-campus management. A single superadmin dashboard gives you consolidated data across every campus, while each school keeps its own isolated environment and custom branding.
Do parents need to install an app? ▾
No. Parents receive WhatsApp messages directly on their phones — no app download needed. They can also access a simple web portal on Growth and Enterprise plans to view attendance and pay fees.
